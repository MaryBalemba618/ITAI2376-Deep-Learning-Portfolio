# My learning journey

At the beginning of this course, I focused on learning the names of frameworks and neural network types. Comparing TensorFlow with PyTorch helped me see that choosing a tool depends on what I need to build. The CNN Cheetah assignment then helped me explain how a network uses patterns, while also thinking about misleading backgrounds and unfamiliar images.

Explaining gradient descent to a younger audience was a useful challenge. I had to understand loss and the learning rate well enough to explain them with a simple example. I carried that idea into LearnPath by keeping lessons short and giving the learner another step after a mistake.

My diffusion midterm made the training process more concrete. I worked through imports, runtime settings and model outputs. The saved loss curve helped me follow progress across 30 epochs. Looking at generated samples also reminded me that a numerical score answers only part of the question.

Arrival added another lesson about evidence. A familiar word or a fluent response can still carry the wrong meaning. I would ask for clarification and keep the original context when I do not have enough support for an interpretation.

For my final project, I brought these lessons into LearnPath. I separated deterministic answer checking from explanation selection and tested retries, hints and rejected expressions. I can show passing software tests and a simulated policy comparison. I still need a real learner study before claiming better learning. Before an investor presentation, I would also need curriculum review, accessibility feedback and a clearer plan for account storage and support.
