# Arrival and Natural Language Processing

Mary Balemba

Assignment: A05

## My reflection

Arrival helped me think about the difference between recognizing words and understanding a message. The tool and weapon confusion showed why context matters. I connected the film to ambiguity, grounding and limited training examples. My strongest takeaway is to ask for clarification when the evidence is weak. The NLP methods in my report are comparisons and proposed tools, rather than a claim that the film used those models.
